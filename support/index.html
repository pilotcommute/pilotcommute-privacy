PilotCommute is a planning tool for airline pilots who commute to base. It is not affiliated with any airline or union.

Frequently asked questions

What does PilotCommute do?

PilotCommute reads your trips from the calendar you pick or from schedule text you paste, suggests commute flights, and helps you set wake and leave alarms. Alarm times are estimates, and alarms may not go off. Always check your crew schedule and set an alarm you control.

How do flight lookups work, and what is the daily limit?

To suggest commute flights and show flight status, the app sends flight numbers, airport codes, and dates through PilotCommute’s server to FlightAware. Flight lookups share a daily limit set by PilotCommute. When it runs out, the app shows a “limit reached” message. Try again tomorrow, or check the flight in your airline’s tools.

Where is my data stored?

Your trips, settings, and alarms are saved only on your iPhone. There is no PilotCommute account. Your pasted schedule text is not sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy for what leaves your iPhone for flight and weather lookups.

How do I delete my data?

Use Reset in the app’s Settings, which cancels PilotCommute alarms and erases all app data on this iPhone. Deleting the app also deletes its data on this iPhone. If you emailed us a bug report, we delete it within 90 days.

How do I report a bug?

Email pilotcommute-bugs@mail.grokbot.com with your app build (shown in Settings), iOS version, and a short description. Do not send passwords or airline portal logins. Only include trip details if you choose to.
