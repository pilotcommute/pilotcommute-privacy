This policy describes how PilotCommute (“the app,” “we”) handles information when you use the iOS app. PilotCommute is a planning tool for airline pilots who commute to base. It is not affiliated with any airline or union.

1. Summary

  • Your settings, trips, pasted schedule text, calendar-derived legs, hotel rewards numbers (if you save them), and alarms are stored on your iPhone.
  • We do not run a PilotCommute account that keeps a copy of your schedule on our servers.
  • To suggest commute flights and show flight status, the app sends flight numbers (or flight idents), airport codes, and dates (schedule time window) to FlightAware through PilotCommute’s Cloudflare relay. Your pasted schedule text and other trip details are not sent for that purpose.
  • To show airport weather, the app sends airport codes to the NOAA Aviation Weather Center (aviationweather.gov).
  • Bug reports leave your device only if you open Mail and tap Send. We delete bug-report email within 90 days.
  • We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for cross-app tracking.

Pasted schedule text is read on your iPhone for parsing. The text you paste is not sent to PilotCommute servers or to FlightAware/NOAA as paste text. Airport codes, flight numbers, and dates derived from it may be used for weather and flight lookups (see §3).

Calendar: If you allow calendar access, PilotCommute reads the one calendar you pick (EventKit, read-only). It looks through event notes on your iPhone to find flights and report times and keeps only those. Other details (crew names, hotels, phone numbers) aren’t saved, shown, or sent.

Under Apple’s App Privacy definition, on-device-only processing is not “collected” for the App Store nutrition label (https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/, accessed 2026-10-27). This policy still describes it for transparency.

FlightAware path (code-checked 2026-10-27): the Cloudflare relay allow-lists only GET /schedules/{start}/{end}?origin&destination… and GET /flights/{ident} (relay/src/index.ts). Weather label shown in-app: Source: NOAA Aviation Weather Center (aviationweather.gov). For general awareness only. Not an official weather briefing. Use official sources for flight planning.

4. How we use information

  • Provide planning features (wake/leave times you configure, commute suggestions, alarms the app tries to set)
  • Call FlightAware (via Cloudflare) and NOAA for the functions above
  • Fix bugs when you email a report
  • Comply with law if required

We do not sell personal information. We do not “share” personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are commonly used under the CCPA/CPRA.

5. Retention and deletion

Your choices: deny calendar/notification/alarm permissions; skip flight/weather features; leave “Include trip details” off (default); delete data in-app or delete the app.

There are no user accounts in the current public design, so Apple’s in-app account-deletion rule (Guideline 5.1.1(v)) does not apply until accounts exist. On-device delete must still work.

7. Children

PilotCommute is intended for working airline pilots, not for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
